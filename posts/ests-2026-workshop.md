A day before the official program of the *21st Annual Conference of the European Society for Textual Scholarship* started, e-editiones held a special pre-conference event, a workshop dedicated to the Polish-speaking community of weathered and prospective *digital* textual editors.

It was a great opportunity to meet representants of major Polish universities and research institutions, as well as all stages in academic careers: from Lublin, Warsaw, Toruń and elsewhere; undergraduate and doctoral students next to established scholars. 

Outline of the workshop already joined the ever growing list at the [TEI Publisher learning hub](https://github.com/eeditiones/workshop#tei-publisher-learning-hub) and slightly polished version of the application we created during the workshop can be found [here](https://dh-lab.teipublisher.com/exist/apps/ests-2026).

Some of the Jinks core developers will stay in Lublin for the duration of the conference, and we hope to continue conversations on the future of digital editions started at the workshop.

Our sincere thanks go to everyone who made this workshop possible:

* local organizers for ESTS 2026 in Lublin, particularly prof. Wojciech Kruszewski and dr Iga Bąska 
* JinnTec
* Biblioteka Uniwersytecka KUL (Catholic University of Lublin University Library)
* CKC UW (Centre for Digital Competence, University of Warsaw)
