## 🗓️ 15th September at 17:00 CEST

Mark your calendars! On 15 September, e-editiones will be celebrating the release of the Open Processing Model (OPM) — a new open-source Python implementation of the TEI Processing Model, bringing the power of TEI processing directly into Python-based Digital Humanities workflows.

### What to expect

Join us for a look at what OPM makes possible for the TEI and Digital Humanities community:

* A first look at the new Python implementation of the TEI Processing Model
* How OPM integrates with existing Python-based DH workflows
* What this means for teaching, research, and scholarly editions
* How implementation experience feeds back into the TEI Consortium and the TEI Technical Council

We'll be joined by members of the project team, including Wolfgang Meier (creator of TEI Publisher) and Magdalena Turska (co-author of the TEI Processing Model), along with the OPM steering committee.

### Why it matters

The TEI Processing Model is a cornerstone of how scholarly editions get rendered and published — but until now, access has been limited outside of specific toolchains. OPM opens that up to the wider Python DH ecosystem, making it easier for researchers, developers, and institutions to build on open standards.
